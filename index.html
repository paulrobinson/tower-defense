How to play
  • Players alternate turns. Attackers go first.
  • On your turn, do one thing: place a piece, or move a piece one cell.
  • Defenders place within 3 cells of the keep.
  • Attackers place on the edge of the board.
  • Moving onto an enemy piece captures it.
  • An attacker moving into the keep deals 1 damage.
  • Attackers win by destroying the keep. Defenders win by destroying every attacking piece.

Click a highlighted empty cell to place. Click one of your pieces to select it, then click a highlighted cell to move.
