    How to play
    • You are the defenders (circles). The CPU plays the attackers (triangles) and goes first.
    • Units are soldiers (sword), artillery (crosshair) and tesla soldiers (lightning bolt).
    • Tesla soldiers within 2 cells of each other link up, shown by a crackling line, and links chain. Each tesla soldier in a group of n attacks with n²: one alone has attack 1, a chain of 3 has attack 9 each. The ⚡ badge shows each one's current attack.
    • You build as many units as you like from the build queue. A soldier takes 1 turn, a tesla soldier 2 and artillery 3. Only the unit at the front is being built. When it's done it appears at the start of your turn on a random cell near the keep. Turn on Repeat to put each finished unit back at the end of the queue.
    • The attackers have 12 units: 3 artillery and 9 soldiers. One enters at random on the edge each turn.
    • Move any or all of your units, then press End turn & fire. Each of your units then attacks one enemy within its range. Artillery that moved this turn holds its fire.
    • Changed your mind? Tap a moved unit, then tap a cell on its path to walk it back. Walking it all the way back lets it move again.
    • Over each enemy you'll attack: the damage it will take when you end the turn, against the HP it has left (e.g. −2–4 of 7 HP). Amber means it might be destroyed; red with ✕ means it will be.
    • Over your units within enemy reach: at risk shows the most they could lose next turn if every enemy that can reach them picked them.
    • On HP bars, solid red marks HP that will be lost and light red HP that might be.
    • When you select a unit, destinations with a red border are within enemy reach.
    • When a unit has more than one enemy in range, select it and tap another enemy to attack that one instead.
    • Damage is random, averaging 3 × attack ÷ defence. A unit at 0 HP is destroyed.
    • Units can't move through other units or the keep.
    • Attackers win by destroying the keep (40 HP, defence 4). You win by destroying all 12 attackers.

    Click a unit to see its moves and attack range; hover a destination to preview the range from there. Dotted lines show who your units will fire at. Grey arrows show the attackers' last moves.
