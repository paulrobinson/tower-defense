How to play
  • You are the defenders. The CPU plays the attackers and goes first.
  • On your turn, move any or all of your pieces up to 2 cells each, then press End turn.
  • A new defender appears next to the keep at the start of each of your turns.
  • Each turn the attackers bring one piece in from the edge (12 in total), then move theirs.
  • Moving onto an enemy piece captures it. Pieces can't pass through other pieces.
  • An attacker that reaches the keep strikes it for 1 damage.
  • Attackers win by destroying the keep. You win by destroying every attacker.

Blue arrows show your moves this turn. Grey arrows and rings show what the attackers did last turn. Press Esc to cancel a selection.
